# 0.10.0 — tmux patience model: solution design

> Companion to `0.10.0-tmux-patience-model.md`. Synthesises a
> five-agent parallel investigation. The agents ran independently and
> **converged on the same diagnosis and the same fix** — that
> convergence is the main evidence this design is correct.

## What the agents converged on

1. **Patience-window audit (Agent A).** Counted **25** distinct patience
   windows (the problem doc listed 11; the real catalogue is larger).
   Found **9 conflict pairs** — windows whose semantics contradict
   under the music-curation workload. Identified **6 load-bearing
   decision points**, dominated by B7's `submitConfirmMs × 5`
   (C1: TUI-parked-paste), §6's `lateGraceMs = 1500` (C2:
   subagent-tool-use latency), and `quiesceMs = 500` (the trigger of
   every downstream race).
2. **Workload characterisation (Agent B).** From 32 real Music
   sessions: MCP cold-start max 33.4 s; subagent duration p90 = 657 s
   (10.9 min), max 949 s (15.8 min); foreground `bin/dl` median 14 s
   p90 109 s; user→first JSONL line p25 = 3.0 s, p90 = 36 s; user→first
   `Agent` tool_use **min 3.2 s, p50 = 6.2 s, max 12.7 s** — **0 of
   183 turns** had a first JSONL line in under 1.5 s. **`lateGraceMs =
   1500 ms` is structurally below the workload floor**, with 99.5 %
   of turns racing the §6 fail-loud against the JSONL tail. Bursts:
   27 % of inbound messages are inside a burst of 2+ within 30 s.
   Wrapped prompt size median 1.2 KB (always above the `[Pasted text
   #N]` collapse threshold). Successful turn duration p90 = 7.2 min,
   max 18.9 min.
3. **TUI signals catalogue (Agent C).** The single most-useful pane
   signal is `esc to interrupt` (A4) — always present when a turn is
   alive (including during subagents), absent on idle. `Press up to
   edit queued messages` (H1) is observable for the C1 trace, but is
   coarser than the JSONL signal. The past-tense verbs (`✻ Cooked for
   Nm Ns`) are **misleading** — they paint mid-turn, between phases,
   and must not be read as "done." The subagent `◯ <name> Nm Ns`
   indicator is NOT per-tick reliable (intermittent rendering).
4. **JSONL + debug-log signals (Agent D).** Three reusable findings:
   (a) **The parser comment claiming `attachment.queued_command` is
   dead in claude 2.1.142 is wrong** — Agent D observed it firing in
   real production traffic 2026-05-20 at 00:46:45, two lines per
   parked paste, when the previously-queued pastes are promoted into
   the next turn. Keep the branch live. (b) **`system turn_duration`**
   is a stronger terminal signal than `stop_reason: end_turn` (lands
   ~50 ms after the terminal assistant line with exact
   `durationMs`/`messageCount`). (c) **The debug log's `[Stall]
   tool_dispatch_start`/`tool_dispatch_end` lines** are the most
   precise progress signal in the system — paired with `toolUseId`
   and exact `durationMs`. Plus: claude itself emits `[WARN]
   stream_idle_partial` at 15 s of API-stream idle (`idleDeadlineMs
   = 300000` — claude internally gives up at 5 min). Sidechain JSONL
   is tailable via `<session-id>/subagents/agent-<aid>.jsonl` (+
   `meta.json` with `agentType`/`description`).
5. **Unified predicate design (Agent E).** From first principles +
   the four facets above, a single state-machine replaces the
   timer-constellation. See §"The predicate" below — Agent E's
   design carries directly with minimal edits.

The structural shift is identical across all five reports:

> Stop letting any single signal **resolve** the question "is the
> turn done/stuck/progressing?" Compute a phase from **all** signals
> continuously. Timers only **demote** phases on silence; they never
> **advance** them. Three wall-clock floors remain as
> unbounded-safety guards. Everything else is event-driven.

## Verified facts the investigation surfaced

Confirmed against real JSONL, real debug logs, and the production
events DB:

- **99.5 % of music-curation turns** have their first JSONL assistant
  line later than `lateGraceMs (1500 ms)`. The C2 fail-loud is
  structurally below the workload floor — the existing 1.5 s grace
  cannot be made coherent by tuning; it must be replaced.
- **100 % of subagent-launching turns** emit `Agent` tool_use **after**
  the §6 grace fires. Today only B10's post-grace re-check saves
  them, and only if the `Agent` line was tailed inside 1.5 s.
- **`attachment.queued_command` IS live** in claude 2.1.142. The
  parser's "dead branch" comment is wrong. The two complementary
  fold signals are `queue-operation enqueue` (live, at paste time —
  carries the polygram `corr-id` in `<polygram-info corr-id="...">`)
  and `attachment.queued_command` (delayed, at next-turn start).
- **The `queue-operation enqueue` content carries our correlation
  token verbatim** — making the W11 / W25 C1 conflict resolvable
  without any new signal infrastructure. polygram already parses
  `queue-operation`; the consumer side just doesn't read the token.
- **The main JSONL is genuinely silent for the full subagent
  duration** (11 min in the B10 case, up to 16 min in the longest
  observed). Sidechain JSONL writes at 0.2–0.4 lines/s sustained,
  with healthy gaps up to 244 s on long Bash and 549 s when waiting
  on an approval. Debug log writes continuously during turns
  (`[API REQUEST]`, `[Stall] tool_dispatch_*`, `Slow render`,
  `Spawning shell`) with max healthy gap ~101 s. **An OR predicate
  across all three channels has no observed false-stuck case at
  ≥ 180 s tolerance.**
- **`system turn_duration`** is the cleanest "turn just ended"
  signal — emitted ~50 ms after the terminal assistant line, with
  exact `durationMs` and `messageCount`. Parser doesn't extract it
  today.
- **`[Stall] tool_dispatch_start`/`end`** lines in the debug log are
  emitted ~50 ms around the JSONL `tool_use`/`tool_result` lines,
  carrying `toolUseId` and exact `durationMs`. A more precise
  outstanding-tool ledger than parsing only the JSONL.
- **`system api_error`** carries `retryInMs`/`retryAttempt`/
  `maxRetries`. polygram should not treat a turn as stuck while
  claude is itself retrying an API call.

## The predicate

### `TurnPhase` enum

The single state per turn. Each consumer subscribes to phase changes;
no consumer races a timer.

```
TurnPhase ::=
  | 'queued'              // in pendingQueue, not yet pasted
  | 'pasted-unconfirmed'  // _pasteAndEnter returned; no JSONL signal yet
  | 'paste-parked'        // queue-operation enqueue for THIS token observed
  | 'submitted'           // JSONL user-message for THIS token observed
  | 'streaming'           // assistant-chunk arriving (heartbeat)
  | 'tool-running'        // ≥1 outstanding tool_use (Bash/Read/Write/...)
  | 'subagent-running'    // ≥1 outstanding `Agent` tool_use (B10)
  | 'bg-shell-running'    // session-level; no in-flight turn; TUI shows `N shell`
  | 'approval-pending'    // approval prompt detected; awaiting respondToApproval
  | 'quiet'               // no active phase + no heartbeat for quietToleranceMs
  | 'wedged'              // 'quiet' has held for quietToWedgedMs with empty outstanding sets
  | 'done'                // terminal JSONL `result` flushed (success/interrupted)
  | 'failed'              // explicit failure (TMUX_SUBMIT_FAILED, kill, drain)
```

Active phases (predicate "progressing"): `pasted-unconfirmed`,
`paste-parked`, `submitted`, `streaming`, `tool-running`,
`subagent-running`, `bg-shell-running`, `approval-pending`. Inactive:
`queued`, `quiet`, `done`, `failed`.

### Inputs and transitions

| Signal | Source | Transition |
|---|---|---|
| `_pasteAndEnter` returns | runner | `queued → pasted-unconfirmed` |
| `queue-operation enqueue` matching turn's `corr-id` | main JSONL | `pasted-unconfirmed → paste-parked` |
| `user-message` reproducing turn's `corr-id` | main JSONL | `pasted-unconfirmed\|paste-parked → submitted` |
| assistant `text`/`thinking` line | main JSONL | heartbeat → `submitted\|tool-running\|... → streaming` |
| `tool-use` (any) | main JSONL | add to outstanding set; phase → `tool-running` or `subagent-running` |
| `tool-result` matching outstanding id | main JSONL | drain set; phase → `streaming` if drained, else stays |
| `[Stall] tool_dispatch_start/end` | debug log | redundant with above + carries `durationMs`; heartbeat |
| `--debug-file` size grew | poll | heartbeat (any phase) |
| capture-pane shows `esc to interrupt` | poll | heartbeat → `streaming` if currently inactive |
| capture-pane shows `Press up to edit queued messages` | poll | fallback `paste-parked` if no JSONL `enqueue` yet |
| capture-pane shows `APPROVAL_PROMPT_RE` | poll | `* → approval-pending` |
| `respondToApproval` lands | internal | `approval-pending → streaming` |
| `system turn_duration` (preferred) OR terminal `result` | main JSONL | `* → done` |
| `system api_error` with `retryAttempt < maxRetries` | main JSONL | heartbeat (claude is retrying — turn is alive) |
| `interrupt()` called | internal | `* → done` (subtype `interrupted`) |
| `kill()` called | internal | `* → failed` |
| no signal for `quietToleranceMs` | timer | active phase → `quiet` |
| `quiet` for `quietToWedgedMs` AND empty outstanding sets AND `submitConfirmed` | timer | `quiet → wedged` |

`quietToleranceMs` (~5 s) and `quietToWedgedMs` (~3 min) are the only
new tunables. Per Agent D's measurement, the OR-across-3-channels
heartbeat never gaps >101 s on healthy traffic; 180 s tolerance has
no false-positives in observed data.

### Per-turn fields

```js
turn.phase                  // TurnPhase enum
turn.phaseSince             // _now() of last phase change
turn.lastActivityAt         // _now() of any heartbeat
turn.submitConfirmed        // true once 'submitted' reached
turn.parked                 // true once 'paste-parked' reached
turn.outstandingTools       // Set<toolUseId> (Bash/Read/Write/Edit/...)
turn.outstandingSubagents   // Set<toolUseId> ('Agent') — existing B10 field
```

`outstandingTools` is the natural generalisation of B10's
`outstandingSubagents`. A 12-min `dl-batch` is structurally the same
shape as a 12-min `Agent` — main JSONL silent, sidechain or debug log
active. Today only `Agent` is tracked.

`parked` is the W11/W25 C1 resolution: a `queue-operation enqueue`
carrying our `corr-id` is **proof of arrival** at the TUI AND **proof
of parking**. `_scheduleSubmitRetries` (the B7 successor) reads it
and stops re-sending Enter — parking is intentional, not an
absorbed-Enter race.

## Window dispositions

Of the 25 windows Agent A catalogued:

| Disposition | Count | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **REPLACED** by the predicate | 13 | `pasteConfirmMs`, `_confirmSubmitViaJsonl` retries, §6 `lateGraceMs`, B10 outstanding-Agent suppression, `_awaitTurnComplete` `quiesceMs`, `_sweepStaleTurns`, the implicit TUI native queue, the per-token paste-confirm/submit-confirm Maps, `_runTurn` 5-way `Promise.race`'s racers (except W1 floor), the reactor's STALL/TIMEOUT decision logic |
| **KEEP-as-absolute-floor** | 3 | **W1** `turnTimeoutMs` (chat `timeout`, now 30 min); **W2** `tmuxRunTimeoutMs` (per `tmux capture-pane` SIGKILL); **W3** `readyTimeoutMs` (startup) |
| **KEEP-as-UX** | 2 | Reactor cascade (rewired to subscribe to `phase-change`); reactor deepening (visual heartbeat only) |
| **KEEP-as-mechanism** (not patience) | 7 | `_pasteAndEnter` per-session lock, `interrupt()`, LogTail polling, approval dedup, applyChain reaction ordering, `_waitForReady` (startup-shape predicate, same idea at different scope), LogTail ENOENT tolerance |

**The constellation collapses from "14+ uncoordinated timers" to "one
predicate + 3 wall-clock floors + a reactor that observes the
predicate."**

## Adversarial scenarios

### The 2026-05-20 C1 trace (TUI parks primary paste while busy)

Today: B7 retries Enter 5×, fails loud `TMUX_SUBMIT_FAILED` on a
paste the TUI legitimately queued.

With the predicate:
1. `_pasteAndEnter` returns → `pasted-unconfirmed`.
2. `queue-operation enqueue` carrying our `corr-id` lands → `paste-parked`.
3. `_scheduleSubmitRetries` reads `turn.parked === true` and **stops retrying**.
4. Prior turn completes; TUI emits `dequeue`; next JSONL line is our `user-message` → `submitted`. Turn proceeds normally.

If no `dequeue` arrives before W1: fails with `TMUX_TURN_TIMEOUT` (not
`TMUX_SUBMIT_FAILED`) — correct attribution (the wedged thing is the
prior turn, not our submission).

### The 2026-05-20 C2 trace (subagent §6 fail-loud at 2 s)

Today: capture-pane wins at 500 ms; `Agent` tool_use lands at 2.5 s;
§6 fires at 1.5 s — fail loud.

With the predicate:
1. `user-message` → `submitted`.
2. assistant `thinking` chunk → `streaming`.
3. capture-pane sees pane quiescent at 500 ms — **does not** win a
   race; just contributes a heartbeat-demoter input. `lastActivityAt`
   is fresh → ignored.
4. JSONL `tool-use Agent` at 2.5 s → `subagent-running`,
   `outstandingSubagents.add(id)`.
5. Pane goes quiet for 11 min. `quiet` transition is **gated on
   `outstandingSubagents.size === 0`** — unreachable.
6. `tool-result` for Agent → drain set → `streaming` → terminal
   `result` (or `system turn_duration`) → `done`.

§6 fail-loud is gone (§6 itself is deleted). The two ordering races
that motivated B10 collapse: there is no race.

### Legitimately wedged tool (Bash hung, no observable output)

1. `phase = tool-running`, `outstandingTools = {bashId}`.
2. No JSONL, no sidechain growth, no debug-log growth (eventually —
   `[Stall] tool_dispatch_*` would have logged the start, then silence).
3. `quiet` transition gated on `outstandingTools.size === 0` —
   unreachable while the tool is outstanding.
4. W1 (turnTimeoutMs) fires at 30 min → fail with `TMUX_TURN_TIMEOUT`,
   payload includes `outstandingTools` so the operator sees which tool
   wedged.

Honest limit: a silent outstanding tool cannot be distinguished from a
slow working tool without external knowledge. W1 is the only honest
upper bound. The predicate's contribution is *naming* this case with
diagnostic detail.

### Burst of 5 messages during a 12-min subagent

1. Msg 1 → primary → `subagent-running`.
2. Msgs 2–5 land. Each autosteers; each `_pasteAndEnter` returns;
   each gets a `queue-operation enqueue` with its `corr-id` → each
   transitions to `paste-parked`. All four alive in parallel.
3. When the subagent returns and the main agent emits its reply, the
   TUI dequeues parked entries. Existing `_enqueuedTurns` FIFO +
   `_foldAutosteer` flow handles routing.
4. None of msgs 2–5 ever enter the `quiet → wedged` path — `paste-parked`
   is an active phase.

Today's `_sweepStaleTurns` (fires at `turnTimeoutMs`) is a no-op for
this scenario — correctly, since the TUI observably has the pastes.

### User `/stop` mid-turn

`interrupt()` → `signalInterrupt` → `_evaluatePhase` → `phase = done`
(subtype `interrupted`). `_runTurn` settles. Reactor sees `done →
interrupted` → DONE state.

### Cold-spawn of music-curation agent (45 s MCP load)

Out of scope of the per-turn predicate (no turn yet). Handled by
`_waitForReady` — same predicate shape (pane stable + READY hint +
debug-log quiet for `readyDebugQuietMs`) bounded by W3
(`readyTimeoutMs`). Already correct (B8). The synthesis is conceptual
unity: both `_waitForReady` and `_evaluatePhase` are *"compute liveness
from many signals; demote on silence; floor on wall-clock"* — the
same idea at two scopes.

## Three-commit migration plan

Each commit is independently shippable and soakable on shumorobot.

### Commit 1 — predicate as observer (low risk)

Add `TurnPhase`, `_evaluatePhase`, `_heartbeat`, the per-turn fields.
Wire into `_handleSessionEvent` and the existing poll loops so the
predicate fires on every signal. **Change no existing control flow** —
predicate emits `phase-change` events for observability only.
Optionally have the reactor subscribe (rewire from `setState` calls
to `phase-change` listener).

Tests:
- Re-play known production traces into the predicate (the 2026-05-20
  C1 + C2, the B10 11-min subagent, the warm-session B7 paste, a
  burst of 5 autosteers) and assert the expected `TurnPhase` sequence.
- Unit-test the demote-on-silence path with a fake clock.

Risk-free: it's purely additive.

### Commit 2 — replace submit confirmation (medium risk)

Replace `_confirmSubmitViaJsonl` with `_scheduleSubmitRetries`. Adopt
`paste-parked` as the gate that suppresses Enter-retries. Delete
`submitConfirmP`/`submitOkP` plumbing in `_runTurn`. Capture-race
gating shifts to the predicate.

Tests:
- Warm-session-large-prompt spike (was the B7 verify path) — green.
- C1 deterministic test: feed a `queue-operation enqueue` with the
  turn's `corr-id`, assert no Enter retries fire, assert turn
  eventually settles when the `user-message` lands.
- B5/B6/B7 unit tests adapted to predicate transitions.

Retires bugs B5/B6/B7 mechanism + the C1 conflict.

### Commit 3 — replace `_runTurn`'s 5-way race (medium risk)

Delete the `Promise.race` over `resultPromise` / `captureP` /
`turnDeadlineP` / `interruptP` / `submitConfirmP`. Replace with phase
subscription (`_onSettle` resolves when phase transitions to
`done`/`failed`/`wedged`). W1 (`turnTimeoutMs`) becomes one
`setTimeout` per turn, not a racer. §6 fail-loud branch deleted
entirely. B10 outstanding-Agent re-check subsumed. `_awaitTurnComplete`
loses its terminal-decision role — keeps polling but only produces
heartbeats + approval-prompt detection.

`_sweepStaleTurns` simplifies to "turns whose predicate has been
`pasted-unconfirmed`/`quiet` longer than autosteerStaleMs without
heartbeat across any channel."

Tests:
- Full `autosteer-tui-real.mjs` suite green.
- Deterministic unit tests for each window-replacement case.
- `_runTurn` LOC drops from ~430 to ~150.

### Plus: secondary findings to land alongside

These are discrete bugs Agent D surfaced during the investigation,
independent of the predicate rewrite:

- **Parser comment fix.** `lib/tmux/session-log-parser.js:376-378`
  claims `attachment.queued_command` is dead in 2.1.142. **It is not.**
  Keep the branch live; update the comment.
- **`system turn_duration` parsing.** Add a `turn-complete` event with
  `durationMs`/`messageCount`. Use as the preferred terminal signal
  (lands ~50 ms after `assistant end_turn`, more reliable than the
  per-line `stop_reason` heuristic).
- **`toolUseResult` rich metadata.** Currently dropped. Surface
  `status`/`agentId`/`agentType`/`totalDurationMs`/`totalTokens`/
  `toolStats` on the `tool-result` event — gives the predicate
  precise subagent timing without re-parsing.
- **`queue-operation` `dequeue` vs `remove` semantics.** Currently
  lumped. Separate: `dequeue` = TUI was idle, paste consumed
  immediately; `remove` = TUI was busy, paste was parked then promoted.
  The C1 detector keys on the latter.
- **Debug-log `[Stall] tool_dispatch_*` parsing.** Optional but
  high-precision. Add a tail-and-parse on top of the existing
  debug-log tail to maintain a `toolUseId → start/end` ledger
  independently from JSONL. Useful for fast detection of approval
  hangs (`Write` `_start` with no `_end` for >60 s).
- **`system api_error` handling.** Treat `retryAttempt < maxRetries`
  as live (don't demote to `quiet`). Surface `[WARN]
  stream_idle_partial` as a "claude itself reports stream stuck"
  diagnostic.

These can land before, alongside, or after the predicate work —
they're independently useful.

## What this does NOT solve

Honest limits:

1. **Genuinely silent hung tool.** Bash spawned a process stuck on a
   syscall with no output. Predicate stays `tool-running` correctly
   (a tool *is* outstanding). W1 (turnTimeoutMs) at 30 min is the
   only honest upper bound. The predicate's contribution: diagnostic
   detail — the failure event carries `outstandingTools`.

2. **UX gap during long subagent runs.** A message queued for 5–10
   min behind a working subagent shows the writing-emoji throughout.
   The predicate enables polygram to *surface* "Queued (subagent
   running)" via the `paste-parked` event — but that's a UX choice,
   not a predicate property.

3. **MCP cold-start latency.** `isolateUserConfig` (rc.26) remains the
   right fix when MCPs aren't needed. The predicate makes
   `_waitForReady` robust to slow startup; it doesn't make startup
   faster.

4. **Claude CLI version drift.** If 2.1.143 stops emitting
   `queue-operation enqueue` with `content`, `paste-parked` becomes
   unreachable and we fall back to today's B7 behaviour. The pin
   regime addresses this; calling it out.

5. **B9 garbled approval-menu detection.** Approval detection is a
   regex on capture-pane bytes; the predicate gates *what we do*
   after detection but not *how reliably* we detect. B9 is orthogonal.

6. **Reactor TIMEOUT (😨) timing.** Still requires *some* wall-clock
   heuristic. Map `wedged → TIMEOUT` and tune `quietToWedgedMs` to
   match the current `freezeMs ≈ 180 s` for UX parity.

## Goal — restated

After this work: when the next workload heavier than chat shows up
(longer agent loops, deeper subagent chains, new tool types), there
is **one place to extend** — the predicate's input set — instead of
N timers to re-tune. The structural debt of "every new workload
finds the next cliff edge" is paid down once.
